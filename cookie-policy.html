Cookie Policy

How this website uses cookies and similar technologies — and how you stay in control.

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. Modern websites also use similar technologies such as local storage, which works in much the same way. On this site we mainly use local storage, and we keep it to a minimum.

2. Your choice comes first

The first time you visit, a privacy/cookie banner appears at the bottom of the screen with genuine options:

  • Accept All — allows strictly necessary storage plus optional analytics.
  • Reject Non-Essential — only strictly necessary storage is used. The site keeps working.
  • Manage Preferences — lets you switch optional categories on or off before saving.

Rejecting non-essential cookies is a real, fully supported choice — nothing breaks and nothing optional loads. You can change your mind at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer of every page.

3. What we use

Strictly necessary (always on)

  • Consent record (seedwel.consent.v1, local storage) — remembers your cookie choice so we do not show the banner on every visit. Kept until you clear your browser storage or choose new settings.
  • Firebase authentication session — only for team members and administrators who sign in. Keeps the signed-in session secure.

Analytics (optional — off unless you allow them)

  • Analytics storage would help us understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, rough location, device type) so we can improve it. No analytics items are set unless you allow them. If we add an analytics provider in future, this policy will list it here first.

What we do not use

  • No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No social-media tracking pixels.
  • No fingerprinting.

4. Third-party content

Some page resources (Google Fonts, Font Awesome icons, Firebase) are loaded from third-party CDNs, and signing in uses Firebase Authentication. These providers may set their own technical cookies when their services are used. We do not control those cookies; see the providers' own policies for details.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

Besides our banner, you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Site settings"). Blocking strictly necessary storage may affect sign-in for team accounts, but the public website will continue to work.
