Privacy Policy

How Seedwel Investment LTD collects, uses and protects personal information.

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Seedwel Investment LTD (“Seedwel”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use seedwel.ltd, our contact and careers forms, and related services.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact enquiries — name, email, phone (optional), service interest and message.
  • Job applications — name, email, phone, position, experience, portfolio link, cover letter and CV file.
  • Technical data — basic browser user-agent on form submit (for abuse prevention), and standard server/hosting logs.
  • Portfolio admin data — used only by authenticated administrators managing the public portfolio.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and deliver requested services.
  • To review job applications and communicate about roles.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and portfolio.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Legal basis & consent

Where forms include a consent checkbox, submitting the form confirms you agree to this policy for the purpose stated on that form. You may contact us to withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing already completed.

4. Storage & security

Form data and portfolio content are stored using Firebase (Google Cloud) with access controls. Contact messages and applications are readable only by authorised administrators. CV uploads are limited in size and type. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure; we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures.

5. Sharing

We may share data with infrastructure providers (for example hosting and Firebase) solely to operate the service, or when required by law. We do not share applicant or client data for third-party marketing.

6. Retention

  • Contact messages — retained while useful for follow-up, then deleted or archived by administrators.
  • Job applications and CVs — retained for recruitment review, typically up to 12 months unless a longer period is needed for an active process or legal reason.
  • Portfolio content — retained until removed by administrators.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal data we hold about you. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Cookies & similar technologies

The public site is primarily static. Third-party fonts, icons and Firebase scripts may set technical cookies or local storage required for authentication on the admin area. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on these pages by default.

9. Children

Our services are directed at businesses and adult professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
