1. Who we are

Seedwel Investment LTD ("Seedwel", "we", "us") is a private company based in Kabwe, Zambia, working across digital solutions (websites, logos, business cards and graphics), online work opportunities, business support and education initiatives. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website (seedwel.ltd).

2. What information we collect

Depending on how you use this website, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details — your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number and (optionally) your business name.
  • Service information — the service you request, details you provide about your business needs, and related notes.
  • Recruitment information — your position of interest, experience level, portfolio/LinkedIn link, cover note and CV/resume file.
  • Account information — if you create a Seedwel account (worker or administrator), your email address and display name.
  • Technical information — your browser's user agent string when you submit a form (used to help troubleshoot submissions).

We do not collect more information than we need, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

3. Why we collect it

  • To respond to your service requests and enquiries.
  • To carry out the work you ask us to do (for example, building a website or preparing branding materials).
  • To manage recruitment: review applications, contact candidates and — only when approved — set up worker accounts and Worker IDs.
  • To keep the website secure and working correctly.
  • To meet our own record-keeping obligations as a business.

4. Service requests

When you submit the Request a Service form, your name, email, phone, business name (optional), selected service and details are saved to our private operations database. Only the Seedwel administrator can read them. We use this information to review your request, contact you about it and manage the work through statuses (New → Reviewing → Assigned → In Progress → Completed). Submitting a request does not create any payment obligation until we agree terms with you.

5. Contact forms

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to our private admin inbox together with the topic you selected, your contact details, your consent record and a timestamp. We use them to reply to your enquiry and, where relevant, to continue a conversation about services. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we will.

6. Job applications and CVs

Because we recruit team members through this website, we handle recruitment data with particular care:

  • Your application (name, email, phone, position, experience, portfolio link and cover note) is stored in a database that only the Seedwel administrator can read.
  • Your CV file is uploaded through a private, signed upload process to our media storage (see Cloudinary below). It is stored as a private, authenticated asset — it is not publicly accessible through a web link and can only be downloaded by the administrator using a short-lived, signed URL.
  • CVs are used only for recruitment: reviewing your application, contacting you and, if you are approved, setting up your worker record.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee employment. If your application is not successful, we retain it only as described in Data retention.
  • You can ask us at any time to delete your application and CV (see Your rights).

7. Accounts (workers and administrators)

Some users (team members and administrators) create Seedwel accounts. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (see Firebase). We store your email address, display name and role. Worker records may include your role, status and — for approved workers — a Worker ID and public verification record that shows only limited verification information (name, Worker ID, role and status). Workers can upload a profile photo for their ID card, which is stored privately in our media storage.

8. Cookies and local storage

Like most websites, we use a small amount of browser storage:

  • Strictly necessary storage — keeps the site working (for example, sign-in sessions managed by Firebase) and remembers your privacy choices.
  • Consent record — your cookie choice is stored locally in your browser so we do not ask you again on every visit.
  • Optional analytics — analytics cookies are only used if you allow them in the consent banner. You can accept all, reject non-essential cookies, or manage your preferences; use "Cookie settings" in the footer at any time to change your choice.

Full details, including the specific items we store, are in our Cookie Policy.

9. Third-party services

We use a small number of reputable third-party services to run this website:

  • Firebase (Google) — database, authentication and hosting of records.
  • Cloudinary — private media and document (CV) storage.
  • Vercel — website hosting and secure serverless functions.
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome (via CDN) — typography and icons.
  • WhatsApp — if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, that conversation is governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

These providers process information on our behalf as processors, or provide their service directly to you. We do not share your information with advertising networks or data brokers.

10. Cloudinary / media storage

Portfolio images, worker ID photos and private CVs are stored in our Cloudinary media environment. Uploads from this website are made through signed, server-side requests — no upload credentials are exposed in your browser. CVs are uploaded as private, authenticated assets; only the Seedwel administrator can generate a short-lived download link. Cloudinary acts as a data processor under its own security standards.

11. Firebase / authentication

Our records (service requests, messages, applications, worker data) are stored in Firebase Realtime Database, and sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. Access is controlled by database rules that keep all submitted records readable only by the Seedwel administrator account. Firebase/Google processes this data under Google's Cloud privacy notice; for European visitors, Google relies on standard contractual clauses where required.

12. Data retention

  • Service requests and contact messages — kept while your request is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Job applications and CVs — kept for the duration of the recruitment process. If you are not hired, we keep your application for a limited period in case a suitable opening arises, and delete it (including the CV) afterwards or when you ask us to.
  • Worker and account records — kept while the account is active and for a reasonable period after it ends, for verification and administrative purposes.
  • Consent records — kept locally in your browser until you clear them.

If you would like anything deleted sooner, contact us (see below) and we will act on your request.

13. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information (including your application and CV);
  • withdraw consent for optional cookies (via the consent banner or "Cookie settings" in the footer); and
  • stop contacting you about services or recruitment.

To exercise any of these rights, email seedwelinvestltd@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp. We aim to respond within a reasonable period and may need to verify your identity before making changes.

14. How we protect information

  • All records are protected by access rules that allow only the authorised Seedwel administrator account to read submitted data.
  • CVs and worker photos are uploaded through signed processes and stored as private assets.
  • The website is served over HTTPS, and administrative pages are not indexed by search engines.
  • We apply security headers and keep the platform's dependencies up to date.

No online service can promise absolute security, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

15. Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults interested in our services or roles. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version, and material changes will be reflected on this page.
