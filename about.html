About Seedwel Investment LTD

Our Story

Practical business support, delivered properly

Seedwel Investment LTD was founded with a simple mission: give Zambian businesses access to professional, well-organised services — from company registration and compliance assistance through to websites, marketing and automation.

We work with entrepreneurs, small businesses and growing organisations across Zambia and beyond. Every engagement is handled with clear documentation, transparent communication and secure handling of client information.

  • Clear scope, timeline and fees agreed before work starts
  • Regular updates while your request is in progress
  • Modern digital tools for secure, organised delivery
  • Support after handover — we don't disappear

What Guides Us

Mission, vision and values

Our Mission

To give every business we serve clear, professional support that removes administrative stress and supports real growth.

Our Vision

To become a trusted long-term partner for Zambian businesses — known for honesty, quality of work and dependable delivery.

Our Promise

We treat every request with care: agreed scope, honest timelines, secure information and communication throughout.

A clear note on government services: Seedwel Investment LTD is an independent private company. We are not ZRA, NAPSA or PACRA and are not affiliated with any government agency. When you request ZRA, NAPSA, PACRA or Workers' Compensation support, Seedwel provides private assistance with the relevant processes — official registrations and certificates are always issued by the responsible government institutions.
