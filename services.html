Our Services

Business Solutions & Technology

Practical services for growing businesses — from registration assistance and compliance support to websites, marketing and automation.

Please note: Seedwel Investment LTD is a private, independent company. We are not the Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA), the National Pension Scheme Authority (NAPSA), the Patents and Companies Registration Agency (PACRA), or any other government body, and we are not affiliated with them. We provide private assistance with the relevant application and compliance processes. Official registrations are issued by the respective government institutions.

Core Services

What we can help you with

Business Compliance

ZRA Tax Services

Assistance with Zambian tax registration and related business compliance processes, so your business can meet its tax obligations with confidence.

  • Guidance on Taxpayer Identification Number (TPIN) registration
  • Assistance with VAT and Turnover Tax registration processes
  • Help preparing and organising required documents
  • Follow-up support and clear explanations at each step
Request Service

ZRA is the Zambia Revenue Authority. Seedwel helps you prepare and complete the process — the authority itself issues registrations and rulings.

Employer Compliance

NAPSA Services

Assistance with National Pension Scheme employer registration and ongoing contribution compliance processes for Zambian employers.

  • Guidance on employer registration with NAPSA
  • Help gathering employee and company details
  • Support with understanding contribution obligations
  • Assistance with Workers' Compensation registration
Request Service

NAPSA is the National Pension Scheme Authority of Zambia. Seedwel assists with the process — memberships and records are issued by NAPSA.

Business Registration

PACRA Registration

Assistance with company and business name registration at the Patents and Companies Registration Agency — the first formal step for many Zambian businesses.

  • Business name and company registration assistance
  • Help choosing a suitable business structure
  • Document preparation and submission support
  • Post-registration guidance (certificates, records, next steps)
Request Service

PACRA is the Patents and Companies Registration Agency of Zambia. Seedwel assists with preparation and filing — certificates are issued by PACRA.
